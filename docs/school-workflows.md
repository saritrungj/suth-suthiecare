# School screening and follow-up

Implementation plan: [school-implementation-plan.md](school-implementation-plan.md).

## Access

| Stable school role | Student scope                                                                 | Allowed health data                                                                                                 |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `admin`            | Assigned school                                                               | General roster and minimal mental-risk summary; no clinical details                                                 |
| `mental_admin`     | Assigned school                                                               | Mental and physical records, family insights, guidance and case/referral work; school administration                |
| `physical_admin`   | Assigned school                                                               | Physical records/assessments only; no mental-risk summary, family insights, guidance or generic case/history bypass |
| `counselor`        | Assigned school, or only the grades chosen for them (none left = no students) | Mental and physical records, family insights, guidance and case/referral work                                       |
| `homeroom`         | Assigned homeroom/co-homeroom classrooms                                      | Mental and physical records for those students                                                                      |
| `subject_teacher`  | Classrooms seated as `subject`                                                | Minimal risk summary and school-configured care advice; never health records                                        |
| `other`            | Assigned classrooms                                                           | Minimal risk summary and school-configured care advice                                                              |
| `dorm_staff`       | Boarders in assigned school                                                   | Minimal risk summary and school-configured care advice                                                              |

Classroom assignments and stable roles are both needed for homeroom insight access. A teacher type label does not grant access. Role revocation applies on the next request. Physical-only exports reuse the same domain checks as normal reads. No legacy account is automatically assigned a new clinical role.

## School workflow page

`/admin/school/workflows`, menu **คัดกรองและติดตามโรงเรียน**:

- **ภาพรวมความเสี่ยง:** student code, classroom, boarding status, latest mental risk/date and school-approved general care advice. No names, scores, answer IDs, individual assessment advice or family details.
- **รอบประเมิน:** academic year/semester, domain, existing form, start/end dates and open/closed state. Progress counts actual submissions inside the configured date window and organization, then restricts the students to the caller's scope. The existing School case list also filters by round/date.
- **คัดกรองซ้ำ:** source assessment, target mental form, eligible responsible staff and due date; pending/overdue/completed/cancelled states. The student sees each pending task under **แบบประเมินที่ต้องทำ** on their account page (`/history/result`, `GET /api/patient/me/assessment-tasks`): form title, due date and a start link only, never the source result, risk or assignee. Submitting the target form closes the task automatically inside the submission transaction (`services/schoolRescreen.js`) when the response belongs to that student, the same organization and is newer than the task. Staff can still link a result by hand (e.g. a submission made without signing in); that path checks the same rule and current classroom/role access. The staff list filters by status chips and search, and marks students without an account, who need the copied form link instead.
- **หอพักและเลื่อนชั้น:** independent boarding metadata, actual graduation date, classroom history and explicit source/destination room mapping into the following academic year. Preview counts before moving an entire classroom cohort. Source populations are snapshotted before updates to avoid double promotion through chained mappings.
- **นำเข้าครู:** Excel/CSV template, dry run, apply and one-time credentials download. Homeroom rows include grade, room and academic year; role and classroom assignment are written together.
- **เกณฑ์รายข้อ:** approved exact question/answer triggers. Grid questions also require an exact row. Backend submission handling evaluates actual answers, ignores client-supplied overrides and preserves the original questionnaire scores. A triggered high-risk marker is stored and returned to the student's result screen. No clinical default is seeded; changing a rule affects future submissions.
- **คำแนะนำและการเก็บข้อมูล:** school-configured advice by risk and retention duration counted from graduation. Preview excludes legal holds. Explicit archive closes School access; it does not delete shared patient accounts, person records or hospital medical records.

Physical fitness can be recorded alongside the existing physical examination and is encrypted in the measurement payload. Boarding metadata is separate from confidential `living_with` family information; verify the roster when setting the new boarding field.

## Onboarding

Student templates add `email`, optional `initial_password` and `is_boarder`. Initial passwords must meet the existing policy; blank values use a generated password. New accounts must change the password on first login, then can verify their imported email by OTP. Email recovery is available only after verification. The account menu also links to verification. Existing linked accounts retain their password and email.

Teacher templates include username, name, email, optional initial password, employee code, stable role and optional grade/room/year. Imports can create `other`, `homeroom`, `counselor`, `subject_teacher` and `dorm_staff`. A subject teacher row may list several rooms of one grade (`1, 2, 3`); a counselor row with grades and no room (`ม.1, ม.2, ม.3`) limits the counselor to those grades, and no grade means the whole school; admin roles use the separate role page. Existing usernames are linked only when they are active members of the selected organization. Imported users receive a dedicated base role with no global rights; new email addresses remain unverified and use the existing staff email-verification flow. Existing account passwords remain unchanged.

Both import screens support up to 3,000 rows and submit in batches of 100; APIs accept at most 200 per request. Student and teacher credentials already returned by successful batches remain downloadable if a later batch fails. General reporting exports omit password values.

## Migration and verification

Apply `node scripts/migrateSchoolModule.js --apply` from `server/`. The runner includes `20261014_school_workflows.sql` after the existing School migrations. The new migration is rerunnable and was applied twice to local MySQL 8.4.8.

Tests include domain/API/export boundaries, boarder scope, exact grid-row risk triggers, server submission overrides, import parsing/validation, screening counts, promotion validation, legal-hold/graduation retention, desktop/mobile accessibility and browser batching for an 800-row synthetic roster. Local MySQL fixtures exercise real screening, teacher/account/classroom, history and retention SQL inside an outer transaction that is rolled back.

Production still requires migration/API/UI deployment, real rosters and teacher assignments, approved screening forms/risk triggers/care advice, and actual graduation dates. Archive is implemented; irreversible deletion and automatic execution of a shared medical-record retention policy are not enabled by this change.

## Behaviour reports, physical follow-up and teacher guidance (2026-10-19)

- **Behaviour reports** (`/admin/school/behavior`, tab "พฤติกรรม" in the student file, API `/school/behavior-reports`): a teacher with `student_behavior.create` reports on a student in their scope (category, concern level, date seen, what they saw). The homeroom teachers of the room and the counselors of the grade are notified (the mental-health admins too when the concern is urgent); the notification holds the student code, room, category and level, never the details. People with `student_behavior.view` and the student in scope read the details and follow-up notes; `update` records follow-ups and status, `assign` hands the report to one of the student's homeroom teachers, counselors or mental-health admins, `delete` cancels it (it stays in the history). A reporter without `view` sees their own reports and statuses but not the follow-up notes. Details and notes are encrypted.
- **Physical risk rules** (workflows page → "ตั้งค่าการดูแล", API `/school/physical-risk-rules`): rules on BMI, weight, height, blood pressure or pulse, optionally limited by age and sex, need `school_workflows.*` plus physical-health scope and an explicit "approved" confirmation. Nothing is seeded. A rule applies to physical records saved after it; when a record meets an active rule the server opens one follow-up item per student and rule (until that item is closed), due after the rule's follow-up days, and notifies the homeroom teachers and physical-health admins.
- **Physical follow-ups** (`/admin/school/follow-ups`, top of the physical tab in the student file, API `/school/follow-ups`): visible to whoever may see the student's physical health; updates (status, due date, assignee among the homeroom teachers and physical-health admins, result) need `student_health.update`. Results are encrypted.
- **Teacher guidance** (workflows page → "ตั้งค่าการดูแล", API `/school/care-guidance/:schoolId`): besides the one-line care advice, four steps per risk level (observe, talk, follow up, refer). Saving does not touch the retention approval. The written steps appear in the risk overview and, for the student's latest mental risk, at the top of the mental-health tab (`/school/students/:id/care-guidance`).
- Migration: `20261021_school_care_phase2.sql` (part of `migrate:school-module`).
- **Risk alerts** (`services/schoolRiskAlerts.js`, settings at workflows page → "ตั้งค่าการดูแล", API `/school/notification-settings/:schoolId`): when a signed-in student's (or a linked person's) mental-health submission is at risk by the server's own result (score rules plus the school's approved answer triggers), the homeroom teachers of their room and the counselors of their grade get an in-app notification; the mental-health admins too if the school turns that on. The default is on, high risk only. The notification holds the student code and name, room, level and the school's one-line care advice; never scores or answers. It runs in the submission transaction behind a savepoint, so a failed alert never loses the submission; a retried submission does not alert twice. Recipients see it only while the student stays in their scope.

## Counseling appointments (2026-10-19)

- **Students** (signed-in `school_student` accounts, panel "นัดหมายรับคำปรึกษา" on `/history/result`, API `/api/patient/school-appointments`): choose a topic, one of the staff who look after them (homeroom teachers of their room, counselors covering their grade, the school's mental-health admins) and one of that person's open slots in the next 30 days, with an optional note (encrypted). At most two upcoming open requests at a time. They follow the status and can cancel before the time. The panel shows the 1323 mental health hotline for urgent help.
- **Staff** (`/admin/school/appointments`, API `/school/appointments`, `/school/appointment-slots`, `/school/appointment-topics`, roles page `school_appointments.*`): `create` opens their own slots (up to 4 hours, 1–10 students, repeat weekly up to 8 times; overlapping open slots are refused; a slot with a booking cannot be closed); `view` lists the appointments booked with them, mental-health admins see the school's; `update` confirms, reschedules to another of their own slots, marks completed (optionally writing a guidance record into the student file when the person also has student health `create`) or not attended; `assign` transfers to another of the student's staff at one of their open slots; `delete` cancels. Topics start from a default list each school edits (topics in use are deactivated, never deleted).
- Notifications: the staff member is told about new requests, student cancellations and transfers to them.
- Tables come from `20261020_school_care_extensions.sql`; no new migration.

## Document library and change history (2026-10-19)

- **Document library** (`/admin/school/documents`, API `/school/documents`, `/school/document-categories`, roles page `school_documents.*`): files (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PNG, JPG up to 10 MB; the type is read from the file signature together with the extension) or http(s) links. Files are stored in the database (`school_document_files`) like the result-page images, and downloaded as attachments with `nosniff` and a sandboxing CSP. Each document has readers: all staff, the mental-health team (staff with mental-health scope), the physical-health team, and/or students. `view` reads what is meant for you; `create` uploads (default readers: all staff); `assign` arranges categories and readers; `update` edits content; `delete` hides (restorable). Students see their school's documents for "นักเรียน" on `/history/result` (API `/api/patient/school-documents`). Default categories: คู่มือ, แบบฟอร์ม, แนวทาง, ประชาสัมพันธ์.
- **Change history** (`/admin/audit`, API `/api/audit-logs`, roles page `audit.view`, CSV with `audit.export`; tab "ประวัติการแก้ไข" in the student file via `/school/students/:id/audit`): who did which action on what and when, with the names of changed fields but never their values. Page reads (`data.read`) are hidden unless asked for. Filters: person, area, date range. The CSV guards against spreadsheet formulas. `requiredAudit` now also keeps record identifiers (report, appointment, follow-up, rule, document ids) in the audit metadata.
- Migration: `20261022_school_care_phase5.sql` (part of `migrate:school-module`).

## Overview report and exports (2026-10-19)

- **Overview report** (`/admin/school/reports`, API `/school/reports/overview`, roles page `dashboard.view`, CSV with `dashboard.export`): counts only, never a student row. Mental-health risk per screening round (each student's latest response inside the round, high / watch / normal plus not assessed), or per month for the last six months when the school has no rounds; and follow-up status for re-screening, overdue guidance follow-ups, referrals, behaviour reports, physical follow-ups and appointments. Staff with a school duty or classroom see their own students; staff without one (the หัวหน้างาน preset) see the whole school. When the viewer has no student-list right, any count from 1 to 4 is shown as "น้อยกว่า 5", and a round's totals are hidden too when one of its counts is, so a hidden figure cannot be worked out by subtraction. The chart uses the app's risk status colours with a legend, direct labels and a table view; periods with a hidden count are starred. Cards link into the matching list only for people who can open it. The population is the current roster, so older rounds count today's students.
- **List exports** (CSV with BOM; spreadsheet formulas are neutralised): behaviour reports (`student_behavior.export`, includes the details the exporter can read), appointments (`school_appointments.export`, without the student's note) and documents (`school_documents.export`). Behaviour and appointment exports are recorded in the change history.

## Mental-health status on the student list (2026-10-20)

- **Student list** (`/admin/school/students`, API `/school/students` with `mental_from`, `mental_to`, `mental_status`): for staff who may see the risk queue (`can_view_risk`, same rule as `/school/risk-summary`; a physical-health-only admin sees none), each student shows the latest mental assessment in the chosen period (default: latest as of today; or a semester, a whole academic year, or a custom range), with its date, form, semester and the class the student was in at the time, plus one status. Filter and counts cover seven statuses: ประเมินแล้ว / ยังไม่มีการประเมิน and คัดกรอง (watch or high risk) / ไม่ต้องคัดกรอง (normal), worked out from the latest result; and ส่งต่อครูแนะแนว / กำลังติดตาม / ส่งต่อ รพ., recorded by teachers. A recorded care step wins over the automatic status until a "ปิดการดูแล" step closes it; the care step in force is the one on or before the period's end.
- **Student file, mental tab**: status today, a form to record a care step (`POST /school/students/:id/care-status`, needs student health `update` within mental scope; note encrypted, audited as `student_care_status.create` with the status only), the care-step history, and every mental assessment grouped by semester with the class at the time, so the record runs from enrollment to graduation across class changes. The class at an assessment comes from `school_enrollment_history`.
- **Semester dates** (`/admin/school/structure` → ตั้งค่าปีการศึกษาและชั้นปี, `PUT /school/schools/:id/academic-years/:year/terms`, needs `school.update`): both semesters' open/close dates per academic year. Assessments outside them, or in years without dates, show only the academic year ("นอกภาคเรียน"). Care steps keep the semester and class they were recorded in.
- Migration: `20261023_student_mental_status.sql` (part of `migrate:school-module`; rollback file included).

## Student file: contacts, BMI-for-age, charts by form, tasks from guidance, hospital referral approval (2026-10-20)

- **Contacts on the general tab** (`GET/PUT /school/students/:id/contacts`): guardian name, relationship and phone, the student's own phone, the emergency contact, and "คำแนะนำการปฏิบัติกับนักเรียนคนนี้". Guardian fields, the student phone and the care note live in the encrypted `school_student_insights` record (merged, so the insights tab keeps its own fields); the emergency contact stays in `school_student_health_profiles` and the physical health-profile save no longer writes it. Anyone with mental or physical health scope for the student sees the contacts; the care note needs mental scope; editing needs `student_health.update` at counselor level (`canCounselStudent`: system admin, unit-wide health staff, counselors and mental-health admins covering the student; homeroom teachers read only). Audited as `student_contacts.update`.
- **BMI-for-age** (`app/src/utils/bmiForAge.js`): WHO Growth Reference 2007, 5–19 years, LMS tables by sex and month, z-score with the WHO restriction beyond ±3 SD. Bands: ผอมมาก < −3, ผอม < −2, สมส่วน ≤ +1, น้ำหนักเกิน ≤ +2, อ้วน ≤ +3, อ้วนมาก > +3 (the last is an extra Thai school band; WHO counts it as obesity). Judged at the age on the measuring day; no birth date, no male/female sex or an age outside 5–19 shows "ประเมินตามเกณฑ์ไม่ได้" with the reason.
- **Mental charts**: a checklist of forms and one small score chart per form. A student opened from the list while a period is chosen shows that period (banner, "ดูทั้งหมด" to clear it).
- **Tasks from guidance** (insights tab): a guidance record is แนะแนวทั่วไป, คัดกรอง or ติดตาม (`record_type`). A screening or follow-up record can open a task in the same request (`task: { target_form_id, assigned_user_id, due_on }`, needs `student_health.assign`), shared rules in `server/services/rescreenTasks.js`. Such tasks have `task_kind` and `guidance_record_id` and may have no source response; completion then accepts any response to the form after the task was made. The insights tab lists the student's tasks (`GET /school/rescreen-tasks?student_id=`, with the result's risk level) with close/cancel. The workflow page keeps the school-wide list; "สร้างงานใหม่" now picks a student and opens the file on the insights tab.
- **Hospital referral approval**: a school unit referring to a `hospital` unit creates a `case_referral_requests` row instead of a referral (one open request per assessment and destination). Approvers (`referral_approvals.view/update`, new roles-page module, school only) are notified and decide at `/admin/referrals/approvals` (API `/api/referral-approvals`); approving creates the referral with the name and form title shared only if the requester had those rights; declining needs a reason the requester sees; the requester (or `referral_approvals.delete`) can cancel. Other referrals are sent directly as before. The school privacy boundary now also checks `source_response_id` in the body, which school staff could not send before (they got 403).
- The student file reloads quietly after a save in a tab, so messages and forms in the open tab stay.
- Migration: `20261024_student_file_actions.sql` (part of `migrate:school-module`; rollback file restores the NOT NULL source response and its foreign key).

## Screening by rounds, follow-up per student (2026-10-20)

- **คัดกรอง = รอบ (กลุ่ม)**: a screening round (`school_screening_rounds`) is for a group: the whole school, or the grade levels chosen when it is made (`school_screening_round_grades`, `grade_level_ids` on `POST /school/screening-rounds`; the list shows them, empty = ทั้งโรงเรียน). A school can make as many rounds per semester as it needs. While a round is active and today is within its dates, it shows under "แบบคัดกรองประจำภาคเรียน" on the student's page (`GET /api/patient/me/assessment-tasks` → `rounds`) for students of its grades, until they send the round's form within the round's dates — the same rule as the staff progress page, which now also counts only the round's grades. The overview report counts each round over its own grades.
- **ติดตาม = รายบุคคล**: tasks in `school_rescreen_tasks` are follow-ups of one student and show under "แบบประเมินติดตาม" on the student's page (`tasks`). The workflow tab is renamed "ติดตามรายบุคคล" and the rounds tab "รอบคัดกรอง". Guidance records are แนะแนวทั่วไป or ติดตาม only; screening is not recorded for one student.
- Migration: `20261025_screening_round_grades.sql` (part of `migrate:school-module`) adds the grade table and turns earlier individual "screening" guidance records and tasks into "follow_up".
- Note for DB tests: `form_responses.submitted_at` is a TIMESTAMP (ends in 2038); run DB test files with `--test-concurrency=1` against one database.

## Shared counseling schedule and guardian accounts (2026-10-20)

- **Shared schedule ("ตารางกลาง")** (`/admin/school/appointments` → ตารางกลาง, `GET/PUT /school/appointment-schedule`, saving needs `school_appointments.update`): one schedule per school — weekdays, start/end time (suggested Monday–Friday 16:20–17:00), minutes per slot and students per slot. Every active counselor of the school (`school_staff_roles.role_code='counselor'`) offers it: the server makes real slots in `school_appointment_slots` for the next 30 days (`schedule_school_id` set), when a student opens the booking page or staff open their slots, at most once a day per school unless the schedule or the counselors change. Booking, confirming, rescheduling and cancelling are unchanged. A counselor closes a single made slot (a holiday) in "ช่วงเวลาว่างของฉัน" and it is not made again; a time already covered by the counselor's own open slot is skipped. Saving a changed schedule (or switching it off) closes the future made slots nobody booked; booked ones stay. Grade-scoped counselors are still only offered to students of their grades. Code: `server/services/schoolAppointmentSchedule.js`; migration `20261026_appointment_schedule.sql`.
- **Guardians — what they can do**: a guardian account (role `school_guardian`, school role `guardian`) signs in on the staff sign-in page (an email code is required in production, so the account needs a real email) and sees only the students linked to it, read-only: general information and contacts (without the staff care note), physical health, mental-health results and care steps (without staff notes or who wrote them), school care advice. Not shown to guardians: the insights tab (family background, guidance records), referrals and referral approvals, follow-up tasks, guardian accounts, audit. All writes are refused.
- **Guardians — creating accounts**: (1) from the student file, general tab → "บัญชีผู้ปกครองสำหรับเข้าระบบ" (`GET/POST/DELETE /school/students/:id/guardians`): create a new account for this student (`school_staff.create`; name, email, username, a suggested temporary password) or link an existing guardian account of the school by username, e.g. for a sibling, and unlink (`school_staff.assign`); (2) as before on the staff and guardians page (`POST /school/guardian-accounts`, several students at once). Staff hand the username and password to the family.
