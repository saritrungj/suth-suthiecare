# Cartoon characters

`care-team-patient.webp` and `care-team-staff.webp` are original 3D cartoon
illustrations generated for SUTHie Care on 18 October 2026 with an AI image
model (OpenAI GPT Image 2.5 via Bloom), from prompts written for this project.
They depict no real person and use no third-party artwork. The files are
trimmed to the characters and saved as transparent WebP.

- Patient sign-in: a doctor, a university student and a nurse holding a heart.
- Staff sign-in: a doctor, a teacher/counsellor and a staff member with a laptop.
